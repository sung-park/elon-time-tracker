Tesla Q1 2020 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: April 29, 2020

## Full Self-Driving (FSD) Subscription
Musk stated the company "will offer Full Self-Driving as a subscription service, but it will be probably toward the end of this year."

## Autonomous Driving Capability
Musk expressed extreme confidence: "it will be possible to do a drive from your home to your office...with no interventions by the end of the year." He clarified this requires further refinement before regulatory approval for fully driverless operation.

## Growth Projections
Musk predicted "40% is the likely number" for compound annual growth rate.

## New Gigafactory Location
The company will "announce the next Giga possibly as soon as a month," with commitment within three months, located in the U.S.

## Solar Roof Production
Musk targeted installing "at a rate of 1,000 a week" by year-end.
