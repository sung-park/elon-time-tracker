Tesla Q2 2020 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: July 22, 2020

## Giga Texas (Austin)
Announced new Gigafactory near Austin, on 2,000 acres. Production focus: Cybertruck, Tesla Semi, Model 3 & Y. "We've already started work on the facility."

## Giga Berlin
Three new factories planned over next 12-18 months.

## Full Self-Driving (FSD)
"I'm very confident about its Full Self-Driving functionality being complete by the end of this year."
"Roll it out later this year" with capabilities for traffic lights, stops, turns.

## Tesla Semi
Production starting "next year" (2021). Initially for internal Tesla freight.

## Battery Technology
Iron phosphate packs for Model 3 in China "in volume production later this year."

## Tesla Insurance
Filing telematics-based product with regulators "very shortly." Target: "handful of states by the end of the year."

## Energy Business
Megapack achieved first quarterly profit. Described as potentially "roughly the same size as Tesla Automotive" long-term.
