Tesla Q3 2020 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: October 21, 2020

## 2020 Delivery Goal
Tesla aims for "500,000 deliveries" by year-end, noting "this goal remains a genuine challenge."

## Berlin and Austin Factories
Expected to "start delivering cars from those factories next year," though "it will start off very slow at first."

## FSD Beta
Released "last night" and planned for wider release "by the end of this year."

## 4680 Battery Cells
Within "a few years," batteries could cost "half as much" with capital expenditures "one-third or less."

## 20 Million Vehicle Target
Target of 20 million vehicles per year by 2030, replacing "1% of the global fleet per year."

## Cybertruck Delivery
"If all goes well, we'll be able to do some Cybertruck deliveries toward the end of next year" (2021) with "probably a lot of deliveries in '22."
