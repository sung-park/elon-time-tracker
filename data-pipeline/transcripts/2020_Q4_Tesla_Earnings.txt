Tesla Q4 2020 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: January 27, 2021

## Full Self-Driving (FSD)
"I'm highly confident the car will drive itself for the reliability in excess of a human this year" (2021).
FSD Subscription: "We will be offering subscription pretty soon in the next month or two."
Open to licensing FSD to other OEMs after demonstrating capability later in 2021.

## Cybertruck
"If we get lucky, we'll be able to do a few deliveries toward the end of this year" (2021), but "I expect volume production to be in 2022."

## Giga Berlin & Texas
"Expect to start production later this year" (2021).

## 4680 Battery Cells
2022 Target: 100 gigawatt-hour capacity.
Three-year timeline for 50% cost reduction and 50% range improvement.

## Tesla Semi
"We simply don't have enough cells" to launch Semi immediately.

## Growth Projections
Targeting 50% long-term growth CAGR; may "materially exceed this in 2021."

## Solar
Plans to become solar market leader.
