Tesla Q1 2021 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: April 26, 2021

## Model Y Prediction
Predicted Model Y will be "the best-selling car or truck of any kind in the world" by 2022.

## Model S/X Ramp
Expected to begin deliveries "probably next month" (May 2021).
Target high-volume production for Model X by Q3 2021.

## Berlin & Austin Factories
"We expect to have initial limited production from those factories this year" (2021).
Plans for "volume production from Texas and Berlin next year" (2022).

## Dojo Supercomputer
Predicted Dojo will be "probably an order of magnitude more efficient" than GPU solutions.
