Tesla Q2 2021 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: July 26, 2021

## Giga Texas & Berlin Model Y
Expects "limited production later this year" of the new Model Y design.

## Cybertruck Production
"Currently in its alpha stages" with plans to move into "beta phases of Cybertruck later this year." Production ramp would follow Model Y completion at Texas facility.

## 4680 Battery Cell Goals
Would likely achieve "above 50% of reaching 100-gigawatt hours a year by the end of next year" (2022).

## FSD Subscription
Launched at $99-$199 monthly.

## Cell Supply Expansion
Suppliers would "double their production in 2022."
