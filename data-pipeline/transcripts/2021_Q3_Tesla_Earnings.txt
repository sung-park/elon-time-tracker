Tesla Q3 2021 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: October 20, 2021

## 4680 Battery Cells
"Early next year" (2022) expected for first vehicle deliveries with 4680 cells.

## $25,000 Model
Still targeting 2023 production start.

## Austin & Berlin Factories
Target to assemble first production cars before year-end 2021.
Both factories targeting "5,000 cars a week as soon as we can" with potential for 10,000 weekly.

## Production Growth
"50% per year" growth target maintained long-term.

## Cybertruck
"Launch by next year" (2022) remains the timeline.
