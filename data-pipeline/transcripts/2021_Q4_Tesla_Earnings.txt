Tesla Q4 2021 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: January 26, 2022

## 2022 Volume Growth
"We do expect significant growth in 2022 over 2021, you know, comfortably above 50% growth."

## Full Self-Driving
"My personal guess is that we'll achieve full self-driving this year" (2022).

## Product Development
"We will not be introducing new vehicle models this year" but will engineer Cybertruck, Semi, Roadster, and Optimus for production "hopefully next year" (2023).

## Optimus Robot
Described as "potentially more significant than the vehicle business over time."

## Chip Shortages
Expects alleviation by year-end 2022 or 2023.
