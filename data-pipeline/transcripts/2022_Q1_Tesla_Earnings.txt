Tesla Q1 2022 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: April 20, 2022

## Production & Growth Targets
Confident in "50% growth in vehicle production in 2022 versus '21" with "reasonable shot at a 60% increase."
Long-term goal: "20 million units a year."
2022 production estimate: "likely over one and a half million cars this year."

## Robotaxi (Driverless Vehicle)
Dedicated robo-taxi "highly optimized for autonomy" with no steering wheel or pedals.
Target: "aspire to reach volume production of that in 2024."

## Cybertruck
"Remain on track to reach volume production of the Cyber Truck next year" (2023).

## Full Self-Driving (FSD)
Believes they "will achieve that this year" (2022).
Over 100,000 beta testers enrolled.

## Optimus Robot
"Ultimately will be worth more than the car business. Worth more than FSD."

## 4680 Battery Cells
Volume production "towards the end of the third quarter and certainly in the fourth quarter" of 2022.
