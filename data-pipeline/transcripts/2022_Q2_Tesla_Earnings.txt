Tesla Q2 2022 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: July 20, 2022

## Cybertruck
"We're hoping to start delivering them in the middle of next year" (mid-2023).

## Factory Ramp
"5,000 cars a week at Austin and Berlin by end of this year or early next year."
"Probably 10,000 cars a week at both locations by end of next year" (2023).
Already achieved "30,000-car weeks" and believes "a 40,000-car week is within reach by the end of this year."

## FSD Beta
"On track to be released for all of North American customers before the end of this year."
Over 100,000 owners have access with 35+ million autonomous miles logged.
