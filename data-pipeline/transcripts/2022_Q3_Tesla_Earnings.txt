Tesla Q3 2022 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: October 19, 2022

## Production Targets
"50% or greater compound annual growth rate" in deliveries.
"1,000 gigawatt hours a year of production capacity in the United States."

## FSD Wide Release
"This quarter, we expect to go to wide release of full self-driving beta in North America."
"Anyone who has ordered FSD beta...will have access...probably about a month from now."

## Cybertruck
"Early production in the middle of next year" (mid-2023).

## Tesla Semi
"We'll be handing over our first production Tesla Semis to Pepsi on December 1."
"Tentatively aiming for 50,000 units in 2024 for Tesla Semi in North America."

## 4680 Battery
"Output is growing rapidly" with production "tripled in Q3."
Targeting "$70 per kilowatt hour cell before any incentive."
