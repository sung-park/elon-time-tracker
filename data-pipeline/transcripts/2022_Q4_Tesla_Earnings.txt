Tesla Q4 2022 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: January 25, 2023

## 2023 Volume Goal
Internal potential is "closer to 2 million vehicles" though guided to 1.8 million.
"If it's a smooth year...we actually have the potential to do 2 million cars this year."

## Cybertruck
Production expected "sometime this summer" (2023). Meaningful volume production targeted for 2024.

## 4680 Cell Production
Achieved 1,000 cars per week by Q4 2022.
100 additional gigawatt-hours of capacity expansion at Giga Nevada.
"Our 2023 goal as a 4680 team is to deliver a cost-effective ramp well ahead of Cybertruck."

## FSD
Beta deployed to approximately 400,000 customers. 100 million miles of autonomous driving data.

## Dojo Computer
Expected operational deployment "later this year" (2023).

## Future Products
"We have other products in development...they're very exciting."
Investor Day scheduled for "March 1."
