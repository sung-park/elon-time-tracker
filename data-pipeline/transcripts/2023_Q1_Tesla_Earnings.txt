Tesla Q1 2023 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: April 19, 2023

## Cybertruck
Details saved for "Cybertruck handover, hopefully around end of Q3" (2023).
Orders in excess of production.

## FSD
"I hesitate to say this, but I think we'll do it this year" (full autonomy in 2023).
Current Hardware 3 can achieve full autonomy across existing fleet.
Over 150 million miles of FSD beta data collected.

## Dojo Supercomputer
Potential for "order of magnitude improvement in the cost of training."
Could become a sellable service similar to AWS.

## Battery
Corpus Christi lithium refinery breaks ground May 2023, commissioning target end of year.
Austin cathode facility: target to produce first material before end of 2023.
