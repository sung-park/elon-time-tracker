Tesla Q2 2023 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: July 19, 2023

## Production Targets
1.8 million vehicles targeted for 2023.
Cybertruck deliveries expected later in 2023, high-volume production next year (2024).

## FSD
Claims FSD will be "better than human by the end of this year" (2023).
Goal: "10 times safer than the average human driver."

## Optimus Robot
Production candidate with all Tesla-designed actuators expected around November 2023.
Robot will perform "useful things" in Tesla factories "sometime next year" (2024).

## Dojo Training Computer
Plans to spend "well over $1 billion" through end of 2024 on Dojo.
Targets 100 exaFLOPs of in-house training capability by end of next year (2024).

## Robotaxi
"Quasi-infinite demand" with manufacturing to achieve "highest units per hour of any vehicle production ever."

## 4680 Battery
Production increasing 80% Q2 over Q1 in Texas.
