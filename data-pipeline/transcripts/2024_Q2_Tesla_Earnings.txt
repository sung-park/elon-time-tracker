Tesla Q2 2024 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: July 23, 2024

## Elon Musk's Future Plans & Timelines

**Full Self-Driving & Robotaxi:**
- "Version 12.5 has five times the parameters of 12.4" and merged highway/city stacks
- Robotaxi unveil moved to October 10th for improvements
- Unsupervised FSD possible "by the end of this year" but "next year seems highly probable"

**Roadster:**
"We've completed most of the engineering...expect to be in production with Roadster next year"

**Optimus Robot:**
- Production Version 1 launching "early next year" for internal Tesla use
- "Several thousand Optimus robots" expected by end of 2025 in factories
- Production Version 2 ramping in 2026 for external customers

**AI & Dojo:**
"We are going to double down on Dojo" to compete with NVIDIA, given GPU supply constraints

**Giga Mexico:**
Postponed pending tariff clarity under potential Trump administration

**4680 Battery:**
- Currently producing "more than 1,400 cybertrucks of 4680 cells a week"
- Dry cathode production launch targeted for Q4 2024

**Distributed Computing:**
Tesla AI5 hardware coming "end of next year" with 100+ hours weekly compute capacity per vehicle

**Valuation Perspective:**
Long-term Optimus valuation could be "several times" the $5 trillion autonomous vehicle estimate
