Tesla Q3 2024 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: October 23, 2024

## 2025 Growth Projection
"Best guess" for vehicle growth is "20% to 30% vehicle growth next year."

## Affordable Model
More affordable vehicle planned for "the first half of next year" (H1 2025) with incentive price below $30,000.

## FSD Safety Timeline
By Q2 2025, FSD will "surpass human driving in safety." May extend to Q3, but "seems extremely likely to be next year."

## FSD Version 13
Expected "5 or 6 fold improvement in miles between interventions" vs version 12.5.

## Ride-Hailing Launch
Plans to roll out in Texas and California "next year" (2025). "We think that we'll be able to have driverless Teslas during paid rides next year."

## Cybercab Production
Targeted to reach "volume production in '26" with "at least 2 million units a year."

## Semi Truck
Pilot builds commence "second half of next year" (H2 2025). "Full production in the first half of 2026."

## Tesla Roadster
Design "close to finalizing" but remains lower priority.

## Energy Storage
Shanghai Megapack factory launching Q1 2025 with 20 GWh initial run rate.

## CapEx
2024 CapEx expected "in excess of $11 billion."
50,000 GPUs deployed in Texas "by the end of this month" (October 2024).
