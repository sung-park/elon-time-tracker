Tesla Q4 2025 Earnings Call Transcript
Date: January 28, 2026

## Elon Musk's Major Announcements

**Model S/X Production End:**
"We expect to wind down S and X production next quarter and basically stop production of Model S and X next quarter."

**Optimus Factory Plans:**
Fremont's S/X line will convert to Optimus robot manufacturing with "a long-term goal of having a million units a year."

**CyberCab Production:**
"We expect to start production in April," with expectations it will eventually exceed all other vehicle production combined.

**Robotaxi Fleet Status:**
"We are well over 500 [robotaxi] vehicles at this point between the Bay Area and Austin," doubling monthly.

**FSD Unsupervised Operations:**
"FSD is 100% unsupervised. We obviously have cars operating with no one in them...in Austin right now."

**Semiconductor Strategy:**
Musk emphasized Tesla must build "TerraFab" — an in-house chip fabrication facility integrating logic, memory, and packaging — to avoid being "supplier limited."

**AI5 Chip Development:**
Spending "pretty much every Saturday" on AI5 chip design, calling it "number one most critical thing."

**Future Vehicle Production:**
"Long-term, the only vehicles that we'll make will be autonomous vehicles with the exception of the next generation Roadster."

## Financial Highlights
- CapEx forecast exceeds $20 billion for 2026
- Automotive margins improved to 17.9%
- FSD paid customers reached 1.1 million globally
